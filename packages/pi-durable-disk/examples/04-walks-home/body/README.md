# The robot as code

The agent writes the robot: `robot/body.mjs` in its workspace, any shape (a dog, a dinosaur: any number of limbs, segments, necks, tails, heads, decorations). `lib.mjs` turns that into MJCF for MuJoCo and `build.mjs` runs it (`node robot/build.mjs`: writes `robot/body.xml` and `robot/body.json`, prints one line). The creature page (04 tab) watches `robot/body.json`, compiles the body in MuJoCo, holds its standing pose for 3 s, shows it (hot reload) and writes `robot/build.json`, which the `build_robot` tool (03-tab-to-cloud/build-robot.ts) reads back to the agent: `Built dino: 32 pieces, 8 motors, 10 spring joints, 10.99 kg, stands 0.389 m high. Tags: ...`, or `Not built: MuJoCo: <its own error>`.

- **Format**: the header of `lib.mjs` is the reference; `dog.example.mjs` and `dino.example.mjs` are complete examples. Units are metres and radians, +x forward, +y left, +z up.
- **Motors and springs**: a joint is a motor (a position motor a policy drives; trainable) unless `passive: true` (a spring: a tail, a neck). Limbs with motors are walked first and a spring above a motor is refused, so the motors are the first joints of the body, in the order the trainer and a policy see them. The simulation gathers the motors from wherever they sit, so a policy sees `free joint + motors`, never the springs.
- **Names the trainer looks for** (written for the author): root body `torso`, a foot geom `<body>_foot` at the tip of each limb tagged `legs` (or any piece marked `foot: true`), keyframes `stand` and `home`. The torso's standing height comes from forward kinematics at the standing pose.
- **Tags** say what a piece is. Free strings in the file, plus a fixed vocabulary the look tools address (`TAG_VOCABULARY`: torso, head, neck, tail, legs, front_legs, back_legs, arms, wings, eyes, jaw, belly, plates, horns, spikes, decoration). `set_color`/`set_shape` take a tag (or `all`), `set_face` draws on the `head` piece (else the torso), `rename` renames. `resize`, `scale`, `set_legs` and `morph` refuse on a code body with one sentence that says to edit the file.
- **Files** in the workspace: `robot/lib.mjs`, `robot/build.mjs`, `robot/examples/{dog,dino}.mjs` (written by `build_robot` from `robot-templates.ts`, generated by `scripts/gen-robot-templates.mjs`), `robot/body.mjs` (the agent's), `robot/body.xml` and `robot/body.json` (built), `robot/build.json` (the page's report). `creature/robot.json` keeps the looks (colour and shape per tag, face, name, skill).
- **Checks**: `tab/test/bodyspec.test.ts` and `codebody.test.ts` (MuJoCo in node), `03-tab-to-cloud/test/robot-tools.test.ts` and `robot-model.test.ts`, and in real Chrome `tab/scripts/check-code-body.mjs` (a dinosaur is built, shown, painted by tag, a broken body is answered with MuJoCo's error and the dinosaur stays, a dog replaces it by hot reload).
