We don't sell to agencies: marketing, design, development or consulting shops that build products for clients. Treat
being one as a disqualifier, even when their clients are fintechs and even when they want to embed or resell our checks.

**Why:** the owner marked an agency lead scored 78 as wrong (2026-10-30): an agency doesn't run its own KYB, its
clients do, and we sell to them directly.

**How to apply:** add a disqualifier criterion "marketing, design, development or consulting agencies"; a lead whose
company is one is disqualified, and the reason names this note.
