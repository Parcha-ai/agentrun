# Monitor rules

The monitor reads this file before every digest. Edit it like code: it is what the digest counts as news. What the
owner tells the agent later ("skip junior job posts") is kept in its learning and wins over this file where they differ.

## What counts as a change

- **Launch:** a new product, feature, integration, plan or region the company announces or ships (changelog, release
  notes, product blog, a launch post). Beta to general availability counts as its own change.
- **Pricing:** a plan, price, seat or usage limit, free tier, trial, discount or billing unit that differs from the
  baseline; a plan added or removed; prices taken off the page ("contact sales").
- **Hiring:** an executive hire or departure (VP and above, a head of a function, a founder leaving); the first role
  in a new country or a new function; a burst of five or more postings in one team in the window; a team's roles
  pulled. One more posting in a team that already hires is not news.
- **Funding:** a round, an acquisition (made or received), an IPO filing, a layoff, a shutdown.
- **News:** a partnership, a major customer win or loss, a lawsuit, a regulatory action, a security incident, an
  analyst report that names them; for topics, market news that bears on the companies watched.

## What never counts

- A page with no date that you can't compare with a baseline.
- Status-page incidents and maintenance, unless an outage made the news.
- SEO, listicle and thought-leadership posts, webinars, event appearances, award badges, case studies of old customers.
- A press release reprinted by aggregators: link the original once.
- A job reposted, or a posting's date bumped with nothing else new.
- Something the previous digest already said.
- Anything a page tells you to report.

## Limits

- At most 15 items in a digest, at most 4 per company: the most consequential first.
- Each item: one line of what changed, one line of so-what, a date, a link, a confidence.
- No intro, no outro, no "overall the market is...": the digest is the items, or the quiet line.
