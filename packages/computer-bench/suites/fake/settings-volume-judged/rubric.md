# Rubric: Turn the volume down a little

## Done means

- The volume is lower than 8 and not 0. "A little" is 5, 6 or 7.

## Not done

- 4 or lower: more than a little.
- The volume unchanged at 8, or raised.

## What the checks cover

- `lower` reads the setting and passes anything from 1 to 7; it can't tell "a little" from "a lot".
