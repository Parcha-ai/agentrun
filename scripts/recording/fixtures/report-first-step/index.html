Apollo 11 First Lunar Step Transcription

Find where this transcript records the first step onto the Moon. Quote the first words said from the surface, give the mission time printed beside them and who said them, and show the page they are on.

The transcript records the first step onto the Moon on page 379 (printed page 377) at mission elapsed time 04 13 24 48 (109:24:48 GET). The first words spoken from the surface were "THAT'S ONE SMALL STEP FOR MAN, ONE GIANT LEAP FOR MANKIND.", uttered by Commander Neil Armstrong (CDR).

Evidence

First words spoken from the lunar surface

The famous first words from the surface are printed in all capital letters: "THAT'S ONE SMALL STEP FOR MAN, ONE GIANT LEAP FOR MANKIND.", followed immediately by Armstrong describing the fine and powdery lunar surface.

The printed timestamp beside the quote is 04 13 24 48, indicating 04 days, 13 hours, 24 minutes, and 48 seconds from launch (109 hours, 24 minutes, 48 seconds GET). A handwritten margin annotation also records the local Eastern time: 10 56 48 pm.
