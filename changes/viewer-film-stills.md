The run viewer draws a look made on stills cut from a film in the film lane, at their seconds (`apps/viewer`). Code that cuts a frame and has the eyes or Jev look at it names it `film.mp4#t=S` in the look's path (several joined by ", "), with the record's own fragment empty, and the vision panel drew every such look as a grey photograph tile. Now a look's seconds are read from its path's fragments when its own fragment is empty: an image look with a second is the film's, marking only its own seconds; a window of video named the same way gets its span. A picture a cited row pairs with a still at the still's own second is drawn as that frame, not as a chapter's keyframe, and a cite of the still with a box (`#t=S&xywh=percent:x,y,w,h`) draws the box over it. A transcription of a piece cut from a film (its path `film.mp4#t=600,660`, as the transcribe skill names each piece) counts its words from the start of the piece; its rows are now shown and matched against cites at the film's seconds, the piece's start plus their own. `?cite=` of a look now lands after the commit that filed it: it landed one commit early, at the `vision.kept` memo (draft 36) that keeps the look's answer before the look is filed, where the panel said the look was not filed yet. `test/fixtures/hear-and-find` is a whole scripted recording of pi-vision's hear-and-find example that the tests read.
