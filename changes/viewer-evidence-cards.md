The chat view's answer draws its evidence: the answer once, then one card per cited look or picture, across all rows, the picture as wide as the pane with every box the cites name drawn on it at once, labelled with the cite's own words. A picture with many boxes gets numbered pins and a legend (boxes with identical words are one entry), and a cluster of small boxes is shown again enlarged with the same pins. A look's finding shows under its card only when it is a sentence. A cite into a video (`video.webm#t=120&xywh=...`) is drawn on the video's own frame at that second, found through the browser's `frame.track`, with a button that plays the clip from there; when the frame cannot be drawn, the card says so and shows the picture the look kept.
