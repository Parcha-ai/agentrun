The phone kind: `src/phone-harness.ts`, provider `phone-harness` in the registry (`providerFor("phone-harness", options)`), rents a temporary cloud Android from the phone harness (phone-harness.com, 5 cents a minute) and drives it over adb. `create(options)` takes the API key as `apiKey` (serve passes `PHONE_HARNESS_API_KEY`), and `adb`, `fetch`, `ledger` and the clock as options. No daemon runs on the phone: `connect()` gives a client in this process that answers `screen`, `input.touch`, `input.keys`, `apps` and `apps.ui_tree` with adb commands, and refuses exec, files, processes, pinch, hold and key combos in words. The phone harness keeps no labels, so the provider keeps them in its `ledger` (`{ put, get, list, remove }`; in memory by default), and list, attach, release and the janitor see only the phones it names. A cloud iPhone is refused in words for now. With no fixture app, the conformance suite's touch, keys and apps cases drive the stock Settings app and read back through `apps.ui_tree`, and a new Core case, `apps.launch-terminate`, which needs the apps group alone, lists, launches and terminates it, and sees the launch in `apps.ui_tree` when the phone has one, else in a screenshot that changed. A run's computers ask for at most the provider's `maxLifetimeMs`. `ComputerErrorCode` gains `phone`. The conformance module reads `COMPUTER_PHONE_HARNESS_*` and `AGENTRUN_COMPUTER_LIVE_PHONE_HARNESS`.
