A run's learning keeps what it can when some of its answer breaks a cap. Before, a ninth new note, a second skill or workflow, more than 64 changed paths, or notes superseding more than 8 between them dropped the whole proposal. Now each cap names the items past it, in the order the answer gave them, and only those are left out: the rest goes on to the grader, and the run's learning names what was left out. A note over its size was already left out alone. The checks also run again until the rest passes, so a cap reached only after a first pass (the supersedes count) leaves out its note too. Only the index's total (more than 200 lines), which no single note is past, still refuses the whole answer. Upgrading: nothing to do.
