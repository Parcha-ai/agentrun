The run viewer's public replay (`apps/viewer`, PANELS.md "What a public replay shows"): the session's timeline sits closed under the title, in a disclosure, The session; opened, a click on any point seeks there and the model's turns scroll to that moment. A Jev decision (a look whose ask is pi-vision's typed questions) reads as one sentence per answer with its score, such as "Jev is 0.95 sure: Is the seal on this page?", with the box it drew over the page in the pages lane and its raw answer behind a click. The developer's counts (paid calls made, unknown, judge asks, model turns, re-asks) join the payments and the cost under Receipts. `?debug=durability` draws all of it as it was.
