Exa and Firecrawl on the run's key. `createWeb` takes `keys.exa` and `keys.firecrawl`, and orders name `"exa"` and `"firecrawl"` for search and fetch. Exa searches through `/search` and reads a page through `/contents`; Firecrawl searches through `/v2/search` and reads a page through `/v2/scrape`. Each request is paid through the run's `Pay` at its list price: `exaFeeOf` (exa.ai/pricing, and Exa's own cost report: a search's text and highlights are in its type's price) and `firecrawlFeeOf` (Firecrawl's credits at the key's Standard plan, $0.00099 a credit; firecrawl.dev/pricing), both read 2026-11-24. They keep the existing providers' contract: tries and receipts, the one search shape, the page cache, and the breaker. A 402 is a quota (off for the run), a 429 a rate limit for its Retry-After, and a refused key (Exa 401 or 403, Firecrawl 401) stops the provider for both tools. A provider's own words never reach the model. Each keyed provider takes typed settings with defaults (`settings.parallel`, `settings.exa`, `settings.firecrawl`; `PARALLEL_DEFAULTS`, `EXA_DEFAULTS`, `FIRECRAWL_DEFAULTS`): search depth, snippet content and length, freshness, domain filters, and main content only. A setting a provider does not take throws in `createWeb`. With no settings, Parallel's requests are unchanged. A keyed fetch provider that gives no page logs `web_fetch_provider_failed` and leaves a turn on the receipt. The parameter inventory is in `packages/web/docs/providers.md`.
