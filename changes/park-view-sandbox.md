While a run is parked, its desktop's or phone's live view says what the run waits for and that the computer is kept, never that it ended. Before, a park closed the viewer page with "This computer's session has ended.", and the console's Screen tab said "This desktop has been released.", though the computer was kept for the resume. Now the viewer page says, for example, "Waiting for your approval. The desktop is kept for 10 minutes, until 14:05.", and the console says "Waiting for your approval. The desktop is kept until 14:05." (or "Waiting for your answer.", "Waiting for you to raise the spend cap."). After the 10 minutes both say "The desktop wasn't kept: 10 minutes passed. A new one starts when the run goes on." GET /v2/runs/{run_id}/computer answers a parked run's kept desktop or phone as `none` with `session: "closed"` and a new `kept: { until }`, then `ended` with the timeout's words once that time passes. Take over stays off while parked.

A sandbox run that parks now keeps its sandbox for 10 minutes too, as a desktop's is kept: the timeline says "The sandbox is kept for 10 minutes while you decide.", an answer within that time goes on on the same sandbox ("It's the same sandbox, as the wait left it."), with its files outside the run folder as they were, and one after it gets a new sandbox that says "The sandbox wasn't kept: 10 minutes passed." The computer janitor ends a kept sandbox at its time across a server restart.
