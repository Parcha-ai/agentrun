The `look` skill teaches a series of images of one scene over time: each place the question asks about is its own small box, never a region spanning several named places; its dates are bracketed and bisected on its own crop, not read from a whole-scene average; a place is dry or gone only when its crop shows none of it, as the eyes say and Jev confirms with a typed question for each crop and date, never from a pixel mask or threshold alone; "against the first image" means present then and absent in some later image, reported at the first absent date with the date it returns; and the place names come from the run's own files.
