The package's own readers (`PACKAGE_KINDS`), `read`'s default: an image fitted or tiled with the sentence that maps it back; a PDF's text with page marks and its scans named, or its pages rendered at the planner's dpi with their text layer beside them; office documents and spreadsheets read with nothing installed, saying what is not shown; a video as frames by seek, or for a lead that takes video the file or a window cut from it. The readers use the providers and the image processor the host passes, as passed; what none of them can do is a typed miss. A result's images in all are held to a quarter of the request (re-encoded smaller, else looked at by the eyes, else withheld), and the eyes are asked within their own image count and request bytes. A cause's first line (`linkSafe`) keeps only a link's origin and path, in a miss, a receipt, an event and a provider's detection.
