A webhook try's bound can be set: `Webhooks`' `timeoutMs` option, and the server's `webhookTimeoutMs` (15 s by default, as before). It is whole milliseconds, refused at zero, in part of a millisecond, or at or past the 60 s claim a try holds its message by, so no try outlives its claim. A try cut by it says the bound it had (`no answer in <n> s`).
