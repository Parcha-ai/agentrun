The file viewer shows images and plays video and audio from the run's file route, which now serves `.webm`, `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.m4a` and `.ogg` inline with their types. Settings → MCP servers has two fal presets, fal (key) and fal (sign in); fal bills your fal account.
