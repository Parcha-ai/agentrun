`agentrun share RUN --to URL` publishes an ended run to a share host and prints its link, `<URL>/r/<id>`. It exports the run again with `--restore`, checks the bundle against `--expect SHA` before anything is sent, and posts the bundle byte for byte to `POST <URL>/r/v2/shares`. A bundle whose sha256 changed since the preview page showed it is refused (exit 1), with words saying to open the page again. The preview page's footer now shows the whole command, `agentrun share RUN --to https://agentrun.ai [--restore IDS] --expect SHA`, and how to sign in to the share host.
- `agentrun login --share URL < TOKEN_FILE` signs in to a share host: the token, read from stdin, is checked at `URL/r/v2/shares` and kept in a `share` entry of the same 0600 config file. The server's `base_url` and `api_key` stay as they were, so every other command still goes where it went, and a later `agentrun login` keeps the share entry. `agentrun logout --share` removes that entry alone; `agentrun logout` still removes the file.
- A share host's refusal is said in the CLI's own words, never its message or a value: a 422 prints its counts by rule (a rule the bundle's own records don't name counts as `unknown`), a 400 says the host does not take the bundle's format, a 413 gives its numbers, a full quota says to take a share down, and a refused token (exit 4) gives the login command. Publishing with no share login for that address refuses before the export, with the same command.
- `agentrun share delete URL` takes a share down with the same token (`DELETE <host>/r/v2/shares/<id>`); its page and bundle then answer 410. A share of another token's (exit 4), one the host does not have (exit 1) and one already deleted (exit 0) are each said plainly.
- `agentrun share RUN` with none of `--to`, `--dry-run` and `--out` still exits 2 and sends nothing; its words now name `--to`. `--to` with `--dry-run` or `--out` is refused.
