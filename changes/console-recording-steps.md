Console: a finished run's recording on the Screen tab has the run's steps beside it. Each step the run took while the file was recorded is listed at its time on the video, in the Timeline tab's words; clicking one moves the video there, and the step the video is at is marked as it plays. A step's time is its timeline time since the row that says the recording began, so a run whose timeline has no such row plays its recording alone, as before. The steps sit beside the player on a wide window and under it on a narrow one.
