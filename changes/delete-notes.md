---
packages: @parcha/agentrun-serve, @parcha/agentrun-client, @parcha/agentrun
kind: breaking
---
An agent learns two things now: its owner's corrections and its compiled workflow. Notes, learned skills, the end-of-run look-back turn and the learning grader are gone.
  - Corrections: an owner's words after a run (a grade's words, or a message after the job's first) become one line of the agent's `corrections.md`, with no model turn. Every run reads the file whole under `# Learned`. It holds at most 2048 bytes; a line that would pass the cap, or that holds a secret, a secret's shape or a hidden character, is refused whole and the run's learning says why. `GET /v2/agents/{agent_id}/learning/corrections` reads it with its ETag, and `PUT` replaces it whole under `If-Match` (CLI: `agentrun learning corrections`, `agentrun learning write-corrections`; client: `learning.corrections`, `learning.writeCorrections`).
  - The compiled workflow is committed as soon as its checks and dry run pass, with no grader: its one promotion check against the agent's own answers decides whether it serves.
  - A grade's `feedback` round is `correction` (was `look_back`). The run's learning carries `corrections` (lines added) and `workflows`; `given`, `read`, `learned`, `pending` notes and the grader's `verdict` are gone, and so are the `proposed` state and the `unparsed`, `propose_failed`, `grader`, `grade_failed` and `reflect_timeout` reasons.
  - Removed routes: `GET .../learning/notes`, `PUT .../learning/files/{file}`, `POST .../learning/workflows/{name}/promote`, the learning batches and the learning evals. `GET .../learning` answers `{agent, main, corrections, workflows, log, counts, impact}`. `approve`, `retire` and `restore` act on a workflow's `README.md`.
  - Removed server options: `--learning-budget` (`AGENTRUN_SERVE_LEARNING_BUDGET`) and `--grade-model` (`AGENTRUN_SERVE_GRADE_MODEL`); `capabilities.learning.grade_model` is gone. A definition's `learning: "notes"` is refused.
  - A native source (Claude Code or Codex, local or through the connector) is handed no learned skills and runs no grader turn: the connector's remote `grade` command is gone. A native target's `capabilities.notes` and `capabilities.skills` are one `capabilities.learning`: whether its runs may learn.
  - Upgrading: read a native target's `capabilities.learning` in place of `notes` and `skills`; move what an agent should always follow into its `corrections.md` (one `- ` line each) with the PUT, and stop reading `learning/notes`, a run's `given`/`read`/`learned`/`pending`/`verdict`, and `feedback: "look_back"`. Existing notes and learned skills on an agent's `main` are no longer served; delete the flags above from your start command.
