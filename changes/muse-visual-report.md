A browser run's replay can show what its browser showed: `chat.html?r=<id>&muse&visual` adds a visual report under the answer in the Muse layout. The recording's own browser video leads it (muted, looped, its long holds skipped, scrubbable), with a chapter strip from the pages the run read that seeks it; the run's screenshots follow as a filmstrip under each page's title (a blank frame, and a picture shown twice, are no tile); the PDFs an artifact holds are first-page thumbnails; a run that skipped its sign-in because it was remembered says so in a tile; and each fact of the answer is a crop of the frame where it was read, boxed by a look that recorded the box (`manifest.facts`, an `agentrun.facts/1` blob: a frame, a box in the frame's own pixels or none, the look's label and receipt), never by the viewer. At a wide window the report breaks out of the chat column, the video large and the fact cards three across; a phone stays one column. A session that had a credential on its page (`frame.gap`, reason `credential_on_page`) has every frame from its first to the gap's end blurred as pixels, never enlarged, in the filmstrip, the crops and the chat's browser card, and the video's playback skips that span; a run with no such gap blurs nothing. Everything is read from the recording's own lines and blobs. Without `&visual` the Muse layout and the default view are as they were.
