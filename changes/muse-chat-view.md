A recording can be opened as a phone chat of the agent's work: `chat.html?r=<id>&muse` is a different layout over the same events, and without `&muse` a recording reads as before. A round avatar at the top names the agent (the host's `reference.label`, else the recording's title) with its state (done, waiting for you, or working); the person's words sit on the right and the agent's replies are bubbles, its thinking and the steps between folded and quiet. The browser is a card inline in the chat: the frame the steps ended on, "Your agent is driving the browser", and a disabled "Take control" (a replay is over); a hold, read from the `browser.sessions` and `browser.handoff` rows alone, reads "Handed the browser over: <what was asked>" and then "Handed back · <outcome>". Where the run waited for a person to sign in, a "Secure Store · Sign in to <site>" card marks it, from the browser's own `browser.profiles` document (the address it gains, then its verification). The answer is the agent's bubble with the files it names as chips, and the host's artifact is a "What I remember" card (times as a person says them, no file names or web addresses). No report page beside it, and every web address is shortened to its host.
