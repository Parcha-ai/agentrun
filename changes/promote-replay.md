A learned workflow's new draft or revision now serves first only after it is replayed on the agent's last 20 graded inputs and its accuracy there is within 2 points of the agent's on the same inputs. Grade a finished run with `PUT /runs/{id}/grade` (`{"correct": true | false, "expected"?: {...}, "source"?: "feedback" | "eval"}`); the grades the agent's runs carry are the inputs a replay uses. A replay starts one run per graded input the workflow fits, with `metrics.path` `replay` and `metrics.replay: {of, outcome}`, at the workflow's cost, and the agent's own runs go on by hand until they end. A replayed input the workflow hands back counts as the agent's recorded answer for it, never as a failure. A replay's answer is right when it matches the grade's `expected` fields, or when it says what the graded answer said and that answer was right. With fewer than 5 graded inputs it fits, the workflow serves on its passing dry run, as before, and its record says so.
`GET /agents/{id}/learning/promotions` lists each decision as `{workflow, digest, at, decided: "promote" | "hold" | "replaying", source: "replay" | "dry_run" | "refit" | "graded", reason, replay?: {of, n, agree, agent_acc, wf_acc, delta, handed_back}, runs?, refit?: {from, guards: [{node, was, threshold, fit?}]}}`. A held revision does not serve; the agent keeps the run. Escalation never holds a workflow. Two things do: the money rule, and graded runs it served more than 2 points under the agent's graded runs by hand, checked once per 20 graded runs it served.
At that check, and when a replay holds, each guarded step's threshold is refit to the cheapest one that keeps the replay's accuracy within the bar, and a `both` guard's `fit` is refit (a logistic over its self and Jev reads) when it has 30 graded rows with 5 wrong. A refit that moves a threshold commits the workflow as a new revision, promoted on the rows it was fit on; its money-rule count starts over.
A run its learned workflow answered alone, on a CLI-shaped record, now reads its booked cost on `GET /runs/{id}` (it read `null`).
