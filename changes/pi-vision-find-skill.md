The `find` skill: the second a thing is first seen in a long video, and its box, found coarse to fine for cents. The transcript's words (the `transcribe` skill) or chapter titles narrow the search to windows, `vision.judge` scores a frame every few seconds, the span is halved to a minute, `vision.see` watches that minute with its sound and names the second, and `vision.judge` checks that frame and the seconds either side and draws the box: `film.mp4#t=1024&xywh=percent:31,6,49,52`. Each step's price, when to stop (found, likely, not found, budget) and one code-mode program a step; no new function.
