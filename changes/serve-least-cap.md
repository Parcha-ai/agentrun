A run paused at its spend cap serves `pending.least_cap_usd`: the least cap that sends the refused request, the very figure
its words name ("Raise the cap to at least $X"), so a client can show it beside `suggested_cap_usd` without parsing the
sentence. It is carried from the refusal that wrote the words (a model request's and a paid call's alike), so the two never
differ, and a pause serves it with its other figures, always.
