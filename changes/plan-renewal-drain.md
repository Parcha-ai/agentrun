A stop no longer loses a ChatGPT plan sign-in to a renewal it cut off: the server's stop, and a runner's (beside the server or in a process of its own). OpenAI may already have spent the refresh token when the stop starts. From the stop on, nothing in that process starts a renewal on its store, and one whose lease was still being taken gives the lease back unsent. A renewal already sent gets up to 25 s from the stop's start to save its new pair before the store closes. A server with no runner beside it (`--no-runner`) now allows for that wait before its stop exits early: its stop's deadline was 15 s. A renewal waiting on another process's lease stops waiting. When a renewal's write-back fails, the new pair is revoked only if the store refused the write unsent (it had closed). On any other failure the row is read back: a pair it holds is kept, and a row still under the renewal's lease is written again. Only when that read fails too is the pair kept unsaved, logged as `renewed_pair_unsaved` with `write=unknown` and the store fault's class. A platform that kills the process sooner than 25 s after SIGTERM can still cut the wait short: give it at least 30 s of grace.
