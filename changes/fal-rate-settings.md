A fal job is priced at the rate its settings are billed at. fal bills some endpoints at a rate that depends on a setting but get_pricing states one rate, so a job at a setting that costs more, or one that leaves out a setting whose default costs more, was held, and booked, under what fal bills: an Ideogram v3 job with no `rendering_speed` renders at BALANCED and was held at half its price. Such a job is now held and booked at get_pricing's live rate times the ratio fal's model page gives its settings (read 2026-11-23), a setting it leaves out at its default, so a price change at fal moves every tier. Where the page and get_pricing disagree, the higher rate is held. A setting value the page names no rate for is refused unsent under a cap, and with no cap it runs booked as an unknown charge.
Every endpoint fal's catalog lists in these families (Ideogram v3 and character, Nano Banana Pro and Gemini 3 Pro Image Preview, Nano Banana 2 and Gemini 3.1 Flash Image Preview, Veo 3 and 3.1, Kling v3, sync-lipsync v2, Fabric 1.0) is decided: priced by its settings, or at one rate. An endpoint in one of them that is not decided, such as a variant fal adds later, is refused unsent under a cap ("fal bills <family> by its settings and <endpoint> has no rate decided here"); before, it was held at get_pricing's one rate, under what fal bills when its settings cost more. Endpoints outside these families are priced at get_pricing's one rate, as before. Ideogram v4 is such a family with no rate decided: its pages bill by `rendering_speed` and megapixels, so each of its endpoints is refused under a cap. Before, its tiling endpoints were held at get_pricing's $0.04 or $0.045 an image. Ideogram v4.5 and v4.6 (billed by quality, and v4.6 by size) and Nano Banana 2.1 (`google/nano-banana-2.1`, by resolution), each with its edit, are such families too: refused under a cap whatever unit get_pricing states them in, so a change of unit at fal can't hold them at one flat rate.
Some of the video endpoints below have no reader for the length fal bills: Veo 3.1 `first-last-frame-to-video`, `reference-to-video` and `extend-video`; Veo 3.1 `fast/image-to-video`, `fast/first-last-frame-to-video`, `fast/reference-to-video` and `fast/extend-video`; Veo 3.1 `lite` and its `image-to-video` and `first-last-frame-to-video`; Veo 3 and its `image-to-video`; Veo 3 `fast/image-to-video`; Kling v3 `pro/text-to-video`, `standard/image-to-video`, `standard/text-to-video` and `4k/image-to-video`; Fabric `text` and `fast`. These endpoints' rates are decided, but their holds aren't yet: under a cap, a job on one is refused until its length can be read.
