A fal job is priced at the rate its settings are billed at. fal bills some endpoints at a rate that depends on a setting but get_pricing states one rate, so a job at a setting that costs more, or one that leaves out a setting whose default costs more, was held, and booked, under what fal bills: an Ideogram v3 job with no `rendering_speed` renders at BALANCED and was held at half its price. Such a job is now held and booked at get_pricing's live rate times the ratio fal's model page gives its settings (read 2026-11-23), a setting it leaves out at its default, so a price change at fal moves every tier. Where the page and get_pricing disagree, the higher rate is held. A setting value the page names no rate for is refused unsent under a cap, and with no cap it runs booked as an unknown charge.
Every endpoint fal's catalog lists in these families (Ideogram v3 and character, Nano Banana Pro, Veo 3 and 3.1, Kling v3, sync-lipsync v2, Fabric 1.0) is decided: priced by its settings, or at one rate. An endpoint in one of them that is not decided, such as a variant fal adds later, is refused unsent under a cap ("fal bills <family> by its settings and <endpoint> has no rate decided here"); before, it was held at get_pricing's one rate, under what fal bills when its settings cost more. Endpoints outside these families are priced at get_pricing's one rate, as before.
`fal-ai/ideogram/v3` (and its edit, remix, reframe, replace-background and generate-transparent): TURBO ×1 (get_pricing's rate), BALANCED, the default, ×2, QUALITY ×3.
`fal-ai/ideogram/character` (and its edit and remix): the page states $0.10 an image at TURBO, $0.15 at BALANCED (the default) and $0.20 at QUALITY; get_pricing states $0.05, which the page names for no setting. Held at the page's rates: ×2, ×3 and ×4 on $0.05. Before, a job was held at $0.05 an image, a third of the default's price.

A resolution an endpoint's page names no rate for (4k on Veo 3 or lite, 1080p on an extend) is refused under a cap.
