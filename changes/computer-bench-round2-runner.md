computer-bench: `needs.apps` entries are now each required, read once per computer through `apps.list` after hello and before setup; an entry that is a list is alternatives, any one of which will do. Who breaks: a task that listed one app's makers' packages side by side (`["com.google.android.deskclock", "com.android.deskclock"]`) now needs all of them and is skipped; wrap them in one list (`[["com.google.android.deskclock", "com.android.deskclock"]]`). The suites are updated.
- A missing app or a failing `needs.capabilities` probe refuses the run as `skipped: needs <id>` (`needs one of a, b`; `needs camera: <why>`), is never retried, and is remembered: the task's other runs are refused without opening a computer. A computer with no apps group is refused in words. `loadTask` refuses an `answer` probe, a probe with placeholders, a method that is not the protocol's (`processes.start` names the wire's `process.*`), and an `exec: deny` oracle call outside ui, input, screen and apps (or `apps.install`), naming the call.
- A computer that stops answering during the agent's turn is infra: the runner pings it on the bench's channel after the agent's part and before the checks, and re-runs the task instead of scoring a dead computer as wrong. After a stop the bench made (serve's cancel lets the computer go) a failed ping keeps `budget`, unless the agent's calls had lost the computer's stream before the stop.
- An `answer` check reads the answer without markdown emphasis and thousands separators (`**103,190.70**` reads `103190.70`). No fuzzy matching and no rounding.
- `verify` reports a skipped task × seed apart (`skipped_because` in verify.json; "X right, Y skipped, Z not right"), and `computer-bench run --verified <verify.json>` runs only the pairs verify proved right, refusing the rest without opening a computer and saying how many it left out.
- `mobileworld.take-selfie` (version 2) probes for a camera through `dumpsys media.camera`, and its oracle presses the camera app's shutter through `ui.act` instead of saving a screen capture.
