The console's pages line up, and a keyboard or a screen reader gets through them: every page's text starts on one gutter with one title style, and the Playground's fields have names.
- Layout: every page uses the same gutter and the page-width tokens, its column starts at the gutter (Settings is no longer centred away from its own tabs), a row of tabs puts its first label on the gutter, and a page title is one style (sans, 24px, semibold) on every page. Mission Control's five counts are one strip, and the Allow button on Settings → MCP servers is as tall as its field.
- Accessibility: the Playground's fields are named by their labels and its switches say which is on; Agents and the Playground have a page heading; the browser's tab names the place ("Runs · AgentRun"); the shell starts with "Skip to the page"; a chart is one Tab stop and the arrows move between its days; Settings' tabs scroll on their own on a phone, never the page; the back controls are at least 24px; a wide table and the Playground's code take the keyboard's focus; and "not offered here" on Settings → Tools passes contrast.
