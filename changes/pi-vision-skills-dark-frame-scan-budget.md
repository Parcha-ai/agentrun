The `report` skill's check refuses a frame, a poster or a thumbnail of a film whose mean brightness is almost black (under `MIN_LUMA`, 16 of 255, measured with ffmpeg's `signalstats` through the shell), naming the file and its brightness, so a chapter frame or a clip poster cut at a film's black opening is cut again a second or two on. A page, a crop and a photo are the document's or the photo's own and are left alone. The `find` skill scans a long film coarsely and keeps its Jev requests in one budget: a coarse pass of 40 frames (it was up to 300) and halving around the candidate, with `maxJudges` (120) in the task counting every request of the scan, the halving and the check (a kept answer is not one), the scan and the halving stopping three short of it so the check always has its three, and a step that reaches it stopping with `stopped: "budget"` and its `requests`. A 47-minute film is searched in about 50 requests, far under a host's cap of 200.
