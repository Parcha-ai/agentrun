The run viewer takes `&hide=durability` (`apps/viewer`, PANELS.md "Embedding"), off by default: a page that replays a run without its kills and resumes, such as a gallery's demo page, draws no part marked `durability`. That is the timeline's process lane with its kills, restores and journal opens, the processes, kills, repeated, asked twice and journal opens counters, the chapters made of process starts and kills, the vision panel's kill and resume badges and its kept, repeated, asked twice and possible payments meters, and the workflow map's kills. A panel marks such a part with the class `durability`. Without the option the viewer is unchanged.
