OpenRouter runs book what OpenRouter charged; routers need no cap or a read-back cost; a provider's error inside a stream is booked by what it reported.
- A request on an OpenRouter model is booked at the cost OpenRouter states in the answer's usage (with your own provider key, its fee plus the provider's charge). The price table's figure is kept beside it (`tableUsd`). A cap still holds each request at list price before it leaves. A stated cost that is missing, not a number, or above that hold is not used: the table's figure is booked instead.
- OpenRouter's routers (`openrouter/auto`, `openrouter/free` and the others) have no price here, because the model they pick is not known in advance. A run with a spend cap is refused before it starts. A run without one is booked at the cost OpenRouter reports, else unknown, never $0.
- A provider's error inside a successful (200) stream (Anthropic's `overloaded_error`, `api_error` or `rate_limit_error`, OpenRouter's mid-stream errors, a Responses `response.failed`) is booked by one rule. If the stream reported tokens before the error, they are booked at list price, or at the cost OpenRouter stated, and the run's cost stays known: before, such a request was booked at the cap's hold and made the run's cost unknown. If it reported no tokens, it is booked as refused at $0, and the run's record keeps the provider's message (`refusal`, or `providerError` beside a booked figure). That holds only while the model generated nothing: once the stream shows the model began its output (a content block, a Responses output item, hidden reasoning included, or a text-tools model's reply the runner had not sent yet), an error with no usage is booked at an unknown cost, never $0.
- Kernel: `startModelProxy` books a cost stated in the answer's usage when its origin is OpenRouter's or serve's translation route's (`statesUsageCost`, `ROUTE_ORIGIN`), on every harness: claude-agent, codex and opencode runs on OpenRouter book what OpenRouter charged too. `Ended` gains `tableUsd` on an answer, `{ end: "answered", label: "error", reason }` and `{ end: "nothing", label: "refused", reason }`. `bookRequest` books the table's figure (rule `answered:over_hold`) when a reported figure is above the hold. The claude-agent policy's usage reads a stream that ends in an `error` event as whole. Routers and in-stream errors are handled on those harnesses too.
