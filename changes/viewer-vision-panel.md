The run viewer gains a vision panel (`apps/viewer`) for a run whose lead reads through eyes. It draws three lanes from the `LookRecord`s a host files into a `vision.*` document (a film read in windows with its frames at their seconds, scanned pages with the cited line's box, a grid of photographs that flip to their labels), the cited account from the run's result (`apollo11.account/1`) with a chip per citation, the receipt of the look a chip names (ask, answer, model, tokens, price, kept), and its meters: looks paid, kept after a kill, repeated, spend (unknown when a look's price is), typed misses by code, and reads cut in flight (observed, from live lines). The photo sort's accuracy against the captions and a weaker eyes model's dropped digits are drawn as the result states them. The shell gives every panel two new view inputs: `ViewContext.cite`, the citation a node with a `cite` prop selects on click (kept in `?cite=`, with no scrub), and `ViewContext.json`, the result's JSON blobs as the shell read them; and a node's `box` prop places it over its parent in percent.
