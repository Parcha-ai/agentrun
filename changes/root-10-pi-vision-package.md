New package, `@parcha/pi-vision`: eyes for pi agents. `read` keeps its name and its arguments and reads images fitted or tiled to the model's limits, PDFs, office documents, spreadsheets and video; what cannot be done is a typed miss, and every look a second model answers is filed with a receipt. It has a host-neutral core, the pi-durable adapter (`./durable`), the Node environment (`./node`), eyes on pi-ai (`./eyes/pi-ai`), the providers (`./providers/<name>`) and `./testing` (fake eyes, fixture builders, the provider conformance suite, the leak check). `look` is declared and not built: it throws `NotBuiltError`.
- It does not depend on the other packages, and a release publishes it with them under the `beta` tag. Its README shows a first read that needs no key and nothing optional installed; its stranger spec (`scripts/stranger/pi-vision.json`) runs that read from the packed tarball in an empty directory with an empty environment, and fails when its output differs from the README's by a byte. Its README and `packages/pi-vision/CHANGELOG.md` have the details.
- It depends on `unpdf`, `@hyzyla/pdfium`, `fflate` and `@xmldom/xmldom`; pi-durable and chord (1.0.4 to 1.2.x), pi-ai (`*`, the host's copy, as Pi's package guidance asks of a package that imports it) and Photon are optional peers. sharp is passed in by a host and is neither a dependency nor a peer, so its LGPL libvips binaries stay out of `docs/dependencies.md`.
- CI gains two jobs: `vision-binaries` installs ffmpeg and poppler and runs the package's `test/binaries` suites, and `pi-vision-pi-floor` runs its build, type tests and suite again through `scripts/pi-leg.mjs` with pi-durable, chord and pi-ai at 1.0.4, one copy of each, the lowest its peers accept. The package develops against 1.1.0, and `pi-1-2` runs it on pi 1.2.0.
