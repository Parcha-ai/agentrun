kind: breaking

The old learning promotion loop is gone. A learned workflow has one path to serving: its one check against the agent's own answers, then promote, or hold and one recompile. Nothing else moves it.
  - DSL: step guards are removed. A node takes no `guard` field (the vocabulary's `GUARD_SIGNALS`, `GUARD_FIELDS`, `GUARDED_NODE_KINDS` and `SELF_CONFIDENCE_KINDS`, the `guardStep` dependency, the `guard` escalation kind and the `$confidence` channel), and `softEscalates` is gone with the refit that read it: a replay obeys every escalate.
  - A promotion record keeps `workflow`, `digest`, `at`, `decided` (`promote`, `hold` or `replaying`), `reason`, `check`, `runs`, `inputs`, `replay_id` and `commit`. `source`, `replay`, `refit`, `checked`, `since`, `held_on` and `skipped_by` are gone, and so are the `queued` and `skipped` decisions: a revision waits for a running check and records nothing. A threshold refit, a graded hold, a graded replay and the walk over a workflow's guards are removed with them.
  - `GET /agents/{agent_id}/learning/loop` returns `{ agent, completed_runs, compiles }`: `guards` is gone. `POST .../revisions/{digest}/replays` takes `inputs: "held"` or a number from 1 to 200 and re-checks the revision on the agent's own completed runs; with no earlier check it picks the agent's last `compile_after` completed runs. The cost series' `replay` is the serving check's numbers, with the agreement check it passed (`agreed` of `alone`, its bar `agree_min`).
  - The compile turn asks only for a new workflow or one revision of a held one. A hand-back, a wrong grade and the money rule's stop ask for no revision. A compile whose drafts all fail their checks is a compile too: it writes a hold whose reason names the failed check, a failed first compile earns the one recompile, and a failed recompile stops; nothing is asked again for that revision. The generality pre-check on Jev (`AGENTRUN_SERVE_LEARNING_GENERAL_THRESHOLD`) is removed: the grader reads every note.
  - Upgrading: delete `guard` from every learned workflow and from any DSL a client authors, and stop reading `guards`, a record's `source`, `replay` or `refit`, and the `queued` and `skipped` decisions; read `check` instead. Check records saved in states this release no longer writes (queued, skipped, ...) are ignored; the revision is checked again.
