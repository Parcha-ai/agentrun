A learned workflow's check and its shadow audit compare two answers by their decision when the definition names no `learning.agree_on`: the replay run, or the shadow, asks the agent's fast model (`model_tiers.fast`, else AGENT_MODEL_FAST) once at its end whether its answer and the other one reach the same decision, and its reply `{"same": boolean, "differs_on": string | null}` decides that pair. Answers equal as JSON still agree with no call, and an `agree_on` revision asks no model. The request runs through the run's own session under its cap and is booked as its spend, so it counts as the check's cost (`check_usd`) or the served run's learning cost. A reply that is not the verdict, a request that failed twice, or a cap refusal counts as a disagreement and says why. The check record keeps `grader` (the model) and each disagreement's `differs_on`. A hold says "its answers reached a different decision from the agent's on k of n (for example: …)", and the console's check line says "compared by the fast model". With no fast model the whole answer is compared as before, and the hold words say a learning agent with no `agree_on` wants one.
