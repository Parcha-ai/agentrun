A runner's graceful `close()` now resolves only after each live run's tool hosts have let go of what they hold, and their charges are booked under the claim the runner still holds. Before, the shutdown sealed the run's journal and aborted its ledger first, so a browser session's release charge was refused ("a tool host's beforeRelease failed: the loop stopped (shutdown)") and a priced computer's last stretch went unbooked. A shutdown's seal now lets the release hooks' commits through until they return; the claim's fence still refuses them once the claim is lost. On the run's cap, money already owed (a computer's time, a browser session's release) is booked after the run's stop, since nothing starts with it; every other step is still refused after a stop.

The wait is bounded, and the close's window now runs from its start:
- A run's release waits for its tool hosts up to a computer provider's cleanup bound (`cleanupMs`, 30 s by default) and a 5 s margin, never past the close's window. A host still going then is cut: each open browser session's time up to the cut is charged (pi-browser's `charge` handle), and the run's root records an `agentrun.release.cut` entry with the bound, the time taken and how many hosts finished. The session's release, when it lands (on this claim or the next one's), bills only the time after that charge. If the charge went past the release's own time, the release bills 0 s, and its `agentrun.browser_charged` entry carries `overbooked_s`. A reserve the cut claim left held is given back at the next claim's reopen. The cut reaches every conversation of the run, its subagents' included. pi-browser's `charge` now skips a session already charged through now, so a cut books no $0 charge under it.
- A lost create's recovery under way in the process (`recoveriesInFlight()`, new in `@parcha/agentrun-computer`, with `recoverCreate` now exported) is waited for at the close: each up to its own bound, or the drain, and the margin. A run's own process (`run-child`) waits for its own recoveries before it exits, and its runner gives that exit the release's bound.
- The close is now the drain plus the lease less its margin (31 s with the image's 6 s drain), or the cleanup bound and 10 s of margins from the close's start, whichever is longer: 40 s by default. Claims are renewed until the close returns. `startRunner` takes `cleanupMs` for a runner whose providers use a longer one.
- The deploy templates give a runner more time to stop: 45 s in `deploy/compose.yaml`, the Helm chart (`terminationGracePeriodSeconds`), Coolify and Dokploy, 45 s of Railway draining (`RAILWAY_DEPLOYMENT_DRAINING_SECONDS`), and 50 s on Fly (`kill_timeout`). A deployment of its own gives a runner at least 45 s, and at least `cleanupMs` + 15 s with a longer cleanup bound.
