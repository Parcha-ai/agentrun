The `report` skill's check holds the page to an allowlist, the same one the recording format uses: only its elements and attributes (plus every `aria-*` and `data-*`), `meta` only as a charset or a viewport, and a `style` attribute read as CSS under the style element's rules (after its comments and escapes: no `url()`, `@import`, `data:`, `javascript:`, `expression(`, `behavior` or `-moz-binding`). An `svg`, a `button`, an `on...` handler or a `srcdoc` is refused, and the refusal names the tag and the attribute. A row of a row-shaped answer may carry `boxes` (the same `{ id, at, look }` as a picture's), and its thumbnail then shows the marks (a small sheet, the marks without a label); the skill says to take the thumbnail at the second of the boxed look, so the box and the frame are one moment. It also says a count over rows is stated as what was logged, never as the film's true total unless every moment was looked at and the page says so, and that a row's text says what the look saw beyond its label. Every finding, row and the answer carries `data-ground`, the key of the `vision.judge` look that judged its texts and each text's claim id (`<look key>:t0 <look key>:t1`), and the check counts a verdict without a look key as not judged: a verdict comes only from the judge's answer, never from the script. When Jev cannot be asked (a cap, an error) the unit gets no verdict and the page's Not checked lists exactly which units were not judged, by count and id, with the reason. A boxed row's thumbnail is about 10rem wide with the row's second and text aligned to its top. The skill also says text stays text (never words as a picture), and a detail nobody asked for (a number painted on a car, a sign's words) goes on the page only when a crop of it was read and agrees.
