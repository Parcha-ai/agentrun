Computerd's grants window shows a Computerd tile for each System Settings pane that gives a grant (Accessibility, which gives Post Event too, and Screen & System Audio Recording) and opens the first pane still missing beside it. Dragging the tile into the pane's list is the whole step: the rows tick as the grants come in, the next pane opens, and the window says every grant is in and closes itself. A click on a tile opens its pane again. The Allow… buttons and the system prompts behind them are gone, and the onboarding window after the grants keeps one grants line with a Show… button. `Computerd --until-granted` no longer writes the LaunchAgent.
