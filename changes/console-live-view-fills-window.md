A run's live view can fill the window. On the Screen tab, the button beside the header (Fill the window) grows the view of the run's browser, desktop or phone to the whole window: the page is drawn as large as it fits at its own shape, with bars where the shapes differ, and clicks, drags and keys still land where they're made on the page. Esc, or the same button, puts it back. The view keeps its connection while it grows and shrinks, and a desktop's or phone's Take over and Hand back stay on the bar above it.
- Upgrading: nothing to do.
