serve's Workspace gains what the runner's claim needs for an Archil run, unused until the cutover wires it. `archilClaim` makes the claim's Archil disk from the server's Archil settings (disk, region, S3 endpoint, optional image) and opens its keys from the store's sealed key rows with the runner's seal key: `archil.api_key` when the claim starts, `archil.disk_token` only when a computer's mount first asks. The values stay in the claim's memory, are listed for redaction, and are dropped at release or when the claim is lost; a missing or unopenable row refuses in words, and nothing reads the environment. `openRunWorkspace` opens a run's Workspace for the claim (Mode 2 on the disk at the run's key, Mode 1 in the run's local folder); in Mode 2 it sweeps the exec spill folder when the run starts and at its end, and what a cleanup leaves or a sweep that fails goes to the run's log, never failing the run. `learningWorkspace` opens an agent's learning folder from the same agent layout (made where absent in Mode 1, never through a symbolic link; at the learning key on the disk in Mode 2, its spill folder swept at its end), for learning's script and the snapshot reads. An Archil Workspace made with its disk's region and a disk-token function now gives the computer's mount (the disk, the region, the run's folder and the token), and attach goes through the shared attach path with the claim's check, asking for no token. That path now takes the claim's epoch in `MountCheck` (`{ readBackOf, epoch }`) and refuses a read-back that is not held exclusively, or is under any other epoch than the claim's, in words that never hold the token.
