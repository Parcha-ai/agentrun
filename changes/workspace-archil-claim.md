serve's Workspace gains what the runner's claim needs for an Archil run, unused until the cutover wires it. `archilClaim` makes the claim's Archil disk from the server's Archil settings row (disk id, region, S3 URL, and an optional exec image, which must be pinned by digest) and opens its keys from the store's sealed key rows with the runner's seal key: `archil.api_key` when the claim starts, `archil.disk_token` only when a computer's mount first asks. The values stay in the claim's memory, are listed for redaction, and are dropped at release or when the claim is lost; a missing or unopenable row refuses in words, and nothing reads the environment. `openRunWorkspace` opens a run's Workspace for the claim (Mode 2 on the disk at the run's key, Mode 1 in the run's local folder); in Mode 2 it sweeps the exec spill folder when the run starts and at its end, and what a cleanup leaves or a sweep that fails goes to the run's log, never failing the run. `learningWorkspace` gives an agent's learning folder from the same agent layout, synchronously: the local folder is made by the first write, level by level and never through a symbolic link, while a read makes nothing (a folder not made yet is NOT_FOUND); on the disk it is at the learning key, its spill folder swept at its end. Learning's script and the snapshot reads go through it. An Archil Workspace made with its disk's region and a disk-token function now gives the computer's mount (the disk, the region, the run's folder and the token), and attach goes through the shared attach path with the claim's check, asking for no token. That path now takes the claim's epoch in `MountCheck` (`{ readBackOf, epoch }`) and refuses a read-back that is not held exclusively, or is under any other epoch than the claim's, in words that never hold the token.
