An MCP tool's setting may pin arguments: `pin: {<argument>: <JSON scalar>}` on `mcp:<server>/<tool>` (never the wildcard), in `PUT /v2/settings/tools` and in an agent's `definition.tool_settings`, 1 to 8 top-level arguments, `null` for one that must be left out. The tool gate refuses a call whose arguments are not as pinned before it is sent or asked about, whatever the permission, and the model reads "mcp:slack/slack_send_message may only be called with channel_id = C0123: its destination is fixed by the agent's settings". A call approved under ask is checked again before it runs. The agent's pin and the server's both hold; two that fix one argument differently refuse every call. A template's `mcp` entry takes `pin: {<tool>: {<argument>: "<value or ${parameters.x}>"}}`: apply writes it, filled, into the agent's tool settings, and a pin whose parameter was not given sets the tool to deny with a `pins.<tool>` warning. `GET /v2/templates` shows each tool's `pin`. `market-monitor` pins its Slack and Gmail sends to its `channel`, and `inbound-lead` its Slack send to `slack_channel`, so a granted send can only go to the destination the owner set at apply.
