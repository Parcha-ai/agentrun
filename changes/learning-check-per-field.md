A learned workflow's check now judges each answer field on its own, against the best known answer that field has: a label, else a stored grade's expected answer, else the agent's own answer. With the shipped bar, a field judged against the agent's answers needs at least `agree_min` (80% by default) of the inputs it answered alone to say what the agent said, and at least `agree_min` of those inputs must be right on every field at once, as before. A field judged against grades needs `agree_min` of those inputs right and at most 2 points under the agent's own accuracy on them, so a correction the agent got wrong is no free pass. A hold names the fields that failed and the inputs each got wrong, and the revise prompt passes that on, so a workflow that is right on one field and wrong on another is told which.
Grades are read for the first time: a grade's `expected` answer is the known answer on its fields, and a grade with no expected answer says whether the agent's answer was right. The check record gains `check.fields` (per field: inputs, right by workflow, right by agent on labelled or graded inputs, which known answer, pass), and each disagreement gains `fields`. `learning.agree_on` refuses paths with a `__proto__`, `constructor` or `prototype` segment.
