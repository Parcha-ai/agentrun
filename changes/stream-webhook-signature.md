A webhook trigger can now take a Sandbar Stream ring's webhook: `signature: "stream"` checks Stream's `X-Stream-Signature` (`t=<Unix seconds>,v1=<hex HMAC-SHA256 of "{t}.{body}">`, keyed by the whole secret Stream shows, within the same 300 s window) and keys each firing by the signed body's `interaction_id`, so a note Stream posts again is one firing. Its event is `interaction`, and `accept`'s `where` reads `/text`: `{"event": "interaction", "where": [{"pointer": "/text", "prefix": "Arun"}]}` wakes the agent only on notes that start with its name.
- Stream makes its own secret, so a stream trigger is made with none: its create answers no `signing_secret` (`secret.set` false, and a `note`), and every request to its hook is the one `401` until the owner gives Stream's secret with `PUT /v2/triggers/{id}/secret` and the body `{"secret": "swh_..."}`, a secret write (the owner's session; the token is `403`). The secret is sealed, cut from what a run shows and an import keeps, and never answered: the route answers its metadata alone. A body that is missing, not a string, not `swh_...` or names another field is `422` under its field, and nothing changes.
- `POST /v2/triggers/{id}/secret` is as it was: no body (any, `{}` too, is `422`), and a new secret is made here and shown once, so the clients' `rotateSecret(id, options)` calls keep their shape. For a stream trigger it is `422` under `source.signature`, naming PUT; PUT is `422` for a trigger whose secret is made here. `features.triggers.webhook.signatures` is `["standard", "github", "stream"]`, and the clients gain `triggers.setSecret(id, {secret})` (TypeScript) and `triggers.set_secret` (Python).
- A PATCH may not move a trigger between a secret made here and Stream's: `422` under `source.signature`, the trigger as it was. Make a new trigger for the other.
- The console's new webhook form offers Sandbar Stream's signature beside the standard and GitHub ones; a stream trigger's page offers Set secret (a password field, sent once and never shown) in place of Rotate secret, and says, until it is set, that every request is refused and where Stream shows its secret.
