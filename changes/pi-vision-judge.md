`vision.judge`, a code-mode global beside `vision.see` (`handle.judge(env, call)`, described to the model by `VISION_JUDGE`): Jev answers typed questions about images in one request: `choice`, `noul`, `score` and `box`, with their probabilities or a box and its confidence. The images (paths read like `see`'s, or image blocks) go in `state.images` by reference, named by their fragments; `examples` are images the questions' instructions or criteria name by reference (classification by example); `state` adds fields of the script's own. A box answer also has `fragment`, its region as `#xywh=percent:` of its image, mapped back through the fit and through a crop's own region. Each judgment is kept as the call's memo before it is filed and told, so a call run again pays and tells once, at the price the judge gives (an unknown one filed as unknown). A host passes Jev as `VisionOptions.jev`: `{ judge, model? }`, `judge` being what `@parcha/agentrun-jev`'s `createJevRunner` returns; pi-vision hands it each image as PNG or JPEG bytes and asks `jev-1.14.0` unless `jev.model` names another. `allow` is asked with `tool: "vision.judge"`.
- `fitImage` takes a Jev-shaped profile: `minEdge` (a smaller image is grown, else padded, and `content` says where its pixels are), `formats` (the types kept as they are) and `baseline` (a progressive JPEG is re-encoded); a processor with only `prepare` is held to them too; `imageHeader` says when a JPEG is progressive. Jev's limits and model are `JEV_*` constants.
- The `look` skill gains Jev patterns as code: classify every page or frame, Jev before and after the eyes, classify by example (words and an example together), presence and box, tiles then a box, and a box's edges checked. Its window cut adds `-fflags +bitexact`, so a WebM window cut again is the same bytes and its paid answer is found again.
