A recompile's cost now sits on the revision it drafted from the moment it is drafted. `GET /agents/{id}/learning/cost` `bill.revisions` listed only the revisions a promotion record names, and a drafted revision gets its first record only when a later run's claim starts its check. Until then (and for good, when no check of it starts: the spend is off, a newer revision replaced it, another revision's check holds the workflow) its compile turn was on no row, an unnamed part of `bill.unattributed_usd`. Such a revision is now a row of its own, `state: "drafted"`, after the recorded ones in compile order, with its compile in `compile_usd` and `unattributed_usd` and `k` its workflow's newest checked `compile_after` (else the default). Once its check starts, its record names it: the same one row, now `checking`, the same compile, never counted twice. A revision a run already ran whose records aged out of the kept feed is not `drafted`: its spend stays the bill's unattributed, as before. Nor is one whose only record the trim dropped (a stop with nothing to check it on runs no replay): the promotion feed now keeps `dropped_at`, the newest `at` of any record a trim dropped, and only a compile whose run started after it is `drafted`. `total_runs` now says it counts every run row, replays included (a replay's `n` is null), and that the jobs are the rows with an `n`. The TypeScript and Python clients' and the console's revision `state` types gain `drafted`.
