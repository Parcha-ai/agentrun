A retired note, skill or workflow draft that waited for its owner's OK waits again when it is restored: a restore gives back the status it had before its retire, and only an approval makes it active. Before, Retire then Restore made a held rule active, and the restore was recorded as the owner's approval. A retire now records the status it took the item from (`retired_from: active | pending` in its front matter), the learning view says it (`retired.from`), and the console's Restore confirm says what the restore does: "It becomes active again" only for an item that was active, else that it goes back to waiting for your OK. A person's edit of a retired note keeps it retired with what its retire recorded, and a run's note may not set `retired_from`. An edit (`PUT /v2/agents/{agent_id}/learning/files/{file}`) that drops or changes a retired note's `status: retired`, or changes its `retired_from`, is now refused (400 `bad_request`, rule `status`); before, an edit that dropped it made the note active.
Upgrading: restore a retired note with `POST /v2/agents/{agent_id}/learning/files/{file}/restore`; an edit that drops or changes its `status: retired` is refused.
