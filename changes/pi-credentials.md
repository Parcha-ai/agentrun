`@parcha/agentrun-pi/credentials`: `FORBIDDEN`, `credentialIn(text)` and `credentialInValue(value)`, the one list of credential shapes (and machine paths) a published or exported run must not hold, and the two checks over it. A scanner of a recording, a store export or a gallery's gate imports it and adds its own exact values. It imports nothing.
