`MissEvent` carries the failed look's `record`: the `LookRecord` the guard, `read`, `vision.see`, `vision.judge` and `vision.transcribe` filed for it (`status` `failed`, a `body` that says why, `facts.key`, and in `facts` what the eyes charged: `cost_usd`, tokens, null when the price is unknown, absent when a request threw). A look the host's sink files nothing for, such as a screenshot no tool named, reached `onMiss` with `receipt: null` and nothing to file, so a failed look the eyes still charged for was missing from the host's looks and its cost. A host that keeps looks files the record like any other; a host that ignores it is unchanged. A miss made before any look (the host's own setup failing) has no record.
