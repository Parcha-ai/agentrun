Redaction now reads text as a terminal shows it as well as with its controls dropped, so a held value split by an ANSI colour code, a title or hyperlink sequence (raw, 8-bit, or escaped in JSON, Python, percent-encoding or an HTML reference) or a backspace overstrike (of any character, one past U+FFFF too) is withheld; characters either side of such a control are normalized together, as a reader sees them, so a precomposed character split into its parts by one is still found. A NUL, a DEL or another control inside a sequence no longer ends it early, a sequence aborted by CAN or SUB shows as nothing, a printable character past ASCII inside a sequence no longer ends it, and a backspace strikes out the last character that takes a cell on screen (a space, a no-break space or a braille blank among them), never a control or a zero-width character. A call's read past its stop counts such sequences as nothing however they are escaped, percent-encoded ones included. Coloured output with no held value is shown as before, and a held value coloured whole is still cut in place. Characters that show as a box or a blank (U+FFFE, U+FFF9-FFFB, the noncharacters U+FDD0-FDEF, private use characters, the braille blank and a lone surrogate) are dropped as a zero-width character is, while a surrogate pair stays. The MCP wire's and the computer keys' scrubbers withhold an answer that still holds a credential in such a form, and the source snapshot and the Git checkpoint block on one.
