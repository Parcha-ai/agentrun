A webhook tells you when a run needs its person and when it ends. `PUT /v2/settings/webhook` with `{url}` sets one URL; `run.needs_input` (the run is `input_required`, `data.pending.kind` `question` or `stopped`) and `run.ended` (completed, failed or cancelled) are POSTed to it as thin `{type, timestamp, data: {run_id, agent_id, status, title, url}}` messages, signed by the Standard Webhooks spec (`webhook-id`, `webhook-timestamp`, `webhook-signature: v1,<base64 HMAC-SHA256 of "{id}.{timestamp}.{body}">`) so any of its libraries verifies them. The `whsec_` secret is made at the first PUT, shown once, and kept sealed under `AGENTRUN_SEAL_KEY`, as tool keys are. A try not answered 2xx in 15 s fails, and no redirect is followed; a failed message is tried again on the spec's schedule cut at about a day, then logged as failed, and a 410 turns the webhook off. Pending messages survive a restart (`DIR/webhook-outbox.json`). `GET /v2/settings/webhook/deliveries` lists the last 50 attempts, `POST .../deliveries/{webhook_id}/redeliver` sends one again under the same `webhook-id`, and `POST .../test` sends a `webhook.test`. The routes are in the OpenAPI document as this server's own (`webhook/1`), and capabilities say `features.webhooks`.
