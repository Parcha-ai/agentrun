The reference entry files every look the vision guard reports into a `vision.looks` document of the run's store, with the screenshot it was of as a blob and its evidence path as its receipt, so the recording's counters read the eyes' looks and cost; a demo passes only its eyes, from its vision lane (`fakes.vision(host, running)` on fakes, the live parts' on `--live`), and the entry calls a demo's own `onLook` and `onMiss` after its own, never replacing them. `vision.filing` is the first of the packages' own kill crossings: the eyes have answered and the look is not yet filed, so the next process files the kept answer and does not ask the eyes again. A planned kill that never lands is reported (`unlanded`) and the command exits 1.
A kill entry takes `hold` and `waitMs` at a parking crossing (`vision.filing`): the nth filing and those after it are parked until `hold` are parked or `waitMs` has passed, then the process is killed, so several answers are kept and none filed. A look is kept with its data set item, its miss when it failed and each frame's second, and every read leaves a live `vision`/`shown` line.
