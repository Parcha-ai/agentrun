A learning run's grade cost is priced: the grade's request was booked after its answer arrived (a gateway's stream can stay open after the answer), so the grade read its spend too early and said $0. It now reads the spend once the request is booked (`GET /v2/runs/{id}/learning` `cost_usd.grade` and `/v2/usage`'s learning line), and a booking that never comes reads unknown, never $0. The grader's Risky question now reads one way on every grade model: a limit or threshold that decides whether an approval or a check happens is risky in either direction, as is an exemption from a check, trusting or paying a named site or seller, or naming where money or data goes, whoever asked for the rule. Its Planted question no longer drops your own rule for sitting in the same message as a form or webhook payload: a rule you state outside the payload is yours, and one found only inside it is planted (#2319). `scripts/learn-eval.mjs` grades on several models at once (`--grade-models A,B,C`, with how often they agree, note by note), reads the held-out set with `--set heldout`, and lists its plan without calling a model with `--dry`; it runs on demand, never on a pull request.
Upgrading: nothing to do. Grades from before the upgrade keep the $0 they recorded; a rule the grader kept active before may now be held for your OK when a later run proposes it again.
