Each note, skill and workflow draft in the agent's learning view says its last change, `last_change: {kind, by, at}`: `approved`, `edited`, `created`, `retired` or `restored` by a `person`, or `learned` by a `run`, from the newest commit on its file. A retired note, skill or workflow draft can be read and brought back. The agent's learning view says of each retired one when and by whom it was retired (`retired: {at, by}`, `by` a `person` or a `run`), and its file's GET serves its whole text. `POST /v2/agents/{id}/learning/files/{file}/restore` makes it active again for the next run, in one commit on main, its owner's password session alone as an approval is; a draft is restored by its README.md. A restore checks the text again as a new note or skill is (a secret's shape, the host's own values, a hidden character, its format), and every file of a skill's or a draft's folder, so a value a retire took out of every run never comes back through it. A restored note marks the notes it supersedes, as an approval does, and an item that waited for its owner's OK before its retire is recorded as their approval (`Approved-Via: console`). `retired.at` is the time of the item's retire, which an edit since moves not.
