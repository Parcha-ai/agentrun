A question waiting under Needs you in the console now puts the answers it suggests on a row of their own, with the answer box and Send on the row under them. Before, the box took whatever width the suggested answers left beside them, and Send could drop to a line of its own. Each card under Needs you also keeps its own height where two sit side by side: a short card no longer stretches to the height of the tall one beside it, with blank space under its content.
