The run viewer draws a transcription (`apps/viewer`, RECORDING.md draft 38). A record filed in a `vision.*` document with tool `transcribe` (or kind `audio`) is drawn in a speech lane of the vision panel as its transcript's rows, each a time span and its words, with no picture; a video that was transcribed is no longer drawn as a window of film. A `#t=` cite of the transcription's look, or of the file it heard (a blob whose SHA-256 is the record's), marks the row its second falls in, and a cited second that falls in no row is said to. A long transcript shows twelve rows, from three before the first cited one. The receipt gives the number of rows, the seconds they span and the provider the transcription went through. A transcription is counted as a look by its key, at its own price, unknown when its price is; its model is listed apart from the eyes, as speech to text.
