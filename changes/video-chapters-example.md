`examples/video-chapters` (`npm run demo:video-chapters`), on the showcase demo template and the reference entry's `demo()`: one agent writes its own code (a `code` tool, pi-codemode's sandbox with `bash` and pi-vision's `vision.see`, following the look skill) to cut a film into windows of about a minute with their sound, ask the eyes about each, and deliver its chapters as `agentrun.cited/1`, each row citing its looks (`#t=`) and a keyframe kept as a blob. On fakes it reads a committed 24 s clip of NASA's 1969 Apollo 11 film with no network (`clip/`, cut by `clip/cut.sh`, source in `ASSETS.md`); `--live` reads the whole film (`sources.json`, checked by SHA-256), with Google's eyes taking each window as video and other eyes taking frames. `--kill` at `vision.filing` and the resume pay for no window twice. `--serve` starts a local server: a page to drop a film on, each run's recording served as it grows (whole lines, `Range: bytes=N-`), a Kill button that kills the run's process, and the viewer; it binds to loopback unless `--host` names another address, and every run has a spend cap.
