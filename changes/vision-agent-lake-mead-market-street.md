Two more questions for the vision agent, each a folder with no code: `examples/lake-mead` (thirteen Landsat images of Lake Mead, 1984 to 2024, committed; when the water fell most and every shoreline it left, boxed and dated) and `examples/market-street` (the 1906 film down Market Street from the Library of Congress, fetched by URL; every vehicle that crosses in front of the camera, to the second, boxed and counted). `ASSETS.md` records each source and its licence as the source page states it.
