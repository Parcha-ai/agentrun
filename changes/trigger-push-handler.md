Every pushed trigger source is served by one handler at `POST /v2/hooks/{trigger_id}` (a source whose settings are a trigger's, the webhook) or `POST /v2/hooks/{source}` (one whose settings are the server's), with no token, in one order: headers, the body cut at the source's limit (413 at once, as declared or as it streams, and the socket closed without reading the rest; 408 when the whole body takes over 30 s), its check against its settings, its handshake, its parse. Every refusal is the one 401. A request that passes its check but is no event its source takes, or names no trigger of it, is answered 200 and said in the log (`hook_unparsed`, `hook_no_trigger`), never swallowed; a hook path that does not decode is said as `malformed_path`. A scope belongs to one trigger: a trigger of a server-wide source on a scope another enabled one of its kind listens on is `409 scope_taken` (`details: {source, scope, trigger_id}`), at a create, an enabling or a move, and an event whose scope more than one trigger listens on (a file edited by hand) fires none and is said (`hook_scope_ambiguous`). Every request's headers must arrive within 10 s. The webhook trigger answers as before. Upgrading: nothing to do.
