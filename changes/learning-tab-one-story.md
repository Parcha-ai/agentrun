The Learning tab tells one story: one run numbering, one takeover, one cheaper ratio and one figure for what learning costs a run, each read from the server once.
  - `GET /agents/{id}/learning/cost` numbers the runs that do a job as the bill does: a run's `n` is its job's number and a replay run's is null (it does no job). `summary.takeover` is the bill's `promoted` run, the first run a learned workflow served, alone or handed back, found by its place in the series, never by a start time two queued runs can share. Each run has `digest`, the workflow revision that ran it (null for the agent's own), `took_over`, true on the first run its revision served (over every run, `limit` aside: where a promotion's flag stands), and `audited`, whether an audit's shadow run checked it, in place of `learned_revision` (the learning folder's commit, which moved with every audit). The CSV has `digest` and `audited` columns. `bill.learning_usd` is what learning booked beyond the jobs' own cost, and `bill.revisions[].served` counts every run a revision served, not only the ones that carry its spend.
  - `GET /agents/{id}/learning`'s `impact` sides are `runs` and `median_duration_s` only: `median_cost_usd`, `cost_runs` and `median_learning_usd` are gone. What a run costs is the cost read's all-in `summary.before` and `summary.after` (a median of the job's own cost left learning out and said a second ratio, -86% beside 2.0x cheaper), and what learning itself costs a run is the bill's `learning_usd` over its runs, which counts the audits' shadow runs and replays a median of mostly-zero rows read as $0.
  - The console's Learning tab: the cost chart, its table and markers number jobs only (the replays between two jobs are spread across the gap, unnumbered, each hovered on its own), and the takeover line is its P marker's line, the P on the run the server marks `took_over`; the cheaper stage says the chart's ratio ("7.7x cheaper", never "About 8x"); the "Since it learned" card has no cost of a run of its own; answer times show to a tenth under 10 seconds, with the change of the times shown; "Learning itself" is the bill's figure a run, booked; the levels and the bill's end labels sit in a gutter right of the plot, off the lines; the bill's dollar axis tops out just above its largest figure; an audited row says so in the table; the audit card counts the audited runs the ledger has not scored yet; and the captions say compile turn and audit shadow run, not look-back, grade or notes.
  - Upgrading: read a cost run's `digest`, `took_over` and `audited` instead of `learned_revision`, allow a null `n` on a replay run, read a run's cost before and after learning from the cost read's `summary` instead of `impact`'s `median_cost_usd`, and what learning costs a run from `bill.learning_usd` instead of `impact.after.median_learning_usd`.
