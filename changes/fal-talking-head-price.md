A run with a spend cap can send fal's talking heads, `veed/fabric-1.0` at `"resolution": "480p"` and `fal-ai/bytedance/omnihuman/v1.5`: each job is held at its voice's length, read before the call from the audio at its `audio_url` alone (never its image, never a length its input states), in whole seconds as fal bills them, at get_pricing's rate. The voice is read https only, redirects included, through the same public-host guard as web_fetch and web_save, and at most 32 MiB. A job is still refused unsent under a cap, as before, when its voice can't be read (an `audio_url` that is not https, a private host, over 32 MiB, a format whose length isn't readable, a timeout), and for Fabric at 720p or with no `resolution`: 720p costs more than the 480p rate get_pricing states, and Fabric's resolution has no default, so send `"480p"`. With no cap such a job runs, booked as an unknown charge, never $0. Fabric's reply states no length, so it is booked at its hold: the ledger books the voice's length, read before the call, not a length fal reports. OmniHuman settles at the length its reply states, in whole seconds. LatentSync, infinitalk and `ffmpeg-api/merge-audio-video` stay unpriceable under a cap.
`publicFetch` and `publicDownload` take `httpsOnly`, which refuses http on the first hop and at every redirect.
