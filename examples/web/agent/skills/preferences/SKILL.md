# Preferences

Use this in every run. How the person wants things done is kept between runs in `preferences.md` in the workspace.

1. At the start of a run, read `preferences.md` when it is there, and follow it.
2. When the person states how they want something done (a format, an order, what to include or leave out, when to ask them), add it to `preferences.md` as one short line in their words, and keep the lines that are still true.
3. Never write a password, a code, a token, an account number or any other secret into it: it says how, never who or what to sign in with.
